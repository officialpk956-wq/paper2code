Design TikTok's For You Page (FYP) — a hyper-personalized short-video feed that hooks users within seconds of opening the app, even with zero prior watch history.

## Business Problem

Short-form video is brutally competitive. The FYP must find your interests within 3-5 videos of opening the app (cold-start), then continuously refine in real time as you scroll. Every swipe is a signal.

## Scale Assumptions

- **1B+ DAU**, infinite scroll consumption
- **~3M videos uploaded daily**
- **Decision latency**: <100ms per video slot
- **Signal latency**: user action visible to model within **1 second**

## Why TikTok is Different

Unlike Netflix (session-based) or YouTube (search-anchored), TikTok generates a **continuous feed from implicit signals only** — no explicit ratings, no search queries. Every play, skip, replay, share, and comment is a signal.

## Architecture

```
User Opens App
  ↓
Pre-Fetched Feed (50 videos ready in buffer)
  ↓ (parallel, background)
Signal Collector
  ├── Play duration (% watched)
  ├── Replay
  ├── Like / Share / Comment
  └── Profile visit / Follow
  ↓ (<1s via Kafka)
Real-Time Feature Pipeline (Flink)
  ↓
User Interest Model (updated continuously)
  ↓
Candidate Generator
  ├── Personalized ANN (user × video embedding)
  ├── Trending (global + regional)
  └── Social Graph (friends' activity)
  → ~1000 candidates
  ↓
Lightweight Ranker (100ms budget)
  ├── Predicted watch time
  ├── Predicted like probability
  └── Predicted share probability
  ↓
Diversity Injector
  ├── Max 3 consecutive same-creator
  └── Freshness boost for new videos
  ↓
Feed Buffer (next 50 videos)
```

## Cold-Start: The Secret Weapon

New users have no history. TikTok's cold-start strategy:

1. **Onboarding interests**: Ask 3 interest categories on sign-up
2. **Location + time signals**: Surface locally trending content
3. **Universal baits**: Highly-liked videos transcend demographics — insert 3-5 of these to calibrate the user
4. **Rapid A/B at user level**: Test 10 different content clusters in the first session and observe which signals fire

:::note
TikTok reportedly builds a usable interest profile within **7-10 videos**. This is the core competitive moat.
:::

## Real-Time Signal Pipeline

The key innovation: signals from user actions feed back into the ranking model **within 1 second**, not hours.

```
User Action → Kafka → Flink (stream processor) → Feature Store
                                                      ↓
                                                  Ranker (next request)
```

## Interview Questions

1. A video goes viral — 10M plays in 30 minutes. How does your system avoid overwhelming a single creator's followers?
2. How do you handle the explore/exploit tradeoff when a user's stated interests conflict with their watch behavior?
3. You discover the model is over-recommending rage-bait content. How do you intervene without destroying engagement metrics?
