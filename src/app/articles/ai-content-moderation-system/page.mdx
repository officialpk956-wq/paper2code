# AI Content Moderation System

<Callout type="tip" title="Key Insight">
Content moderation is a high-stakes, adversarial game. You cannot rely on a single LLM to catch everything. A robust moderation system uses an ensemble of specialized, multi-modal classifiers feeding into a rules engine, backed by a human review queue for edge cases.
</Callout>

## Multi-Modal Classification

When a user uploads a post containing an image and text, the payload is fanned out to multiple specialized microservices:
1. **Text NLP Models:** Lightweight models (like RoBERTa) trained to detect hate speech, harassment, and spam.
2. **Vision Models:** CNNs or ViTs trained to detect NSFW imagery, graphic violence, or weapons.
3. **Audio Models:** Speech-to-text transcription followed by text analysis, combined with acoustic models detecting screams or gunshots.
4. **Hash Matching:** Using perceptual hashes (like PhotoDNA) to instantly match uploads against known databases of illegal content (like CSAM).

## The Decision Engine

The classifiers do not make the final call; they simply return confidence scores (e.g., `NSFW: 0.89`, `Toxic: 0.12`).

These scores are passed to a **Decision Engine** (a rules-based system) which applies business logic. The system sets two critical thresholds:
- **Auto-Approve Threshold:** If all scores are below $0.4$, publish the post instantly.
- **Auto-Reject (Block) Threshold:** If a severe violation (like NSFW) is above $0.95$, block the post and warn the user.
- **The Gray Zone (Human Review):** If a score falls between $0.4$ and $0.95$, the AI is uncertain. The post is routed to a human moderation queue.

## Human-in-the-Loop & Appeal Flows

AI models lack nuance regarding satire, cultural context, and complex political speech. Therefore, the "Gray Zone" queue is handled by human reviewers. 

Furthermore, users must have an **Appeal Flow**. If a user's post is auto-rejected by the AI, they can appeal the decision. This appeal goes directly to a human. 

Critically, the decisions made by humans in both the Gray Zone and the Appeal Flow are fed *back* into the training dataset. This creates a continuous active learning loop that makes the AI smarter over time.

## Adversarial Robustness

Malicious actors constantly try to bypass moderation systems.
- **Text Obfuscation:** Typ0s, leetspeak (h@te), or inserting invisible Unicode characters. The NLP models must be trained with aggressive data augmentation to handle noise.
- **Image Perturbation:** Adding adversarial noise to an image that changes the Vision model's prediction without changing how the image looks to a human.
- **Concept Drift:** New slang or harmful memes emerge daily. The system must be retrained continuously to catch emerging trends.
