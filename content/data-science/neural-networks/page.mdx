# Introduction to Neural Networks

Neural networks are a set of algorithms, modeled loosely after the human brain, that are designed to recognize patterns.

<Callout type="info" title="What is a perceptron?">
A perceptron is the fundamental building block of a neural network. It takes multiple inputs, multiplies them by weights, adds a bias, and passes the result through an activation function.
</Callout>

## The Architecture

<SVGDiagram src="/placeholder-nn.svg" alt="Neural Network Architecture" caption="A standard feed-forward neural network" />

The standard architecture consists of:
1. **Input Layer**: Receives the initial data.
2. **Hidden Layers**: Performs computations and extracts features.
3. **Output Layer**: Produces the final prediction.

## Activation Functions

Activation functions introduce non-linearity into the network, allowing it to learn complex patterns.
